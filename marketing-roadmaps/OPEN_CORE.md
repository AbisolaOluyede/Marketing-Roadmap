# Open-core product model

## Free, open-source core

The MIT-licensed core includes:
- All 14 marketing roadmaps and their 211 topics.
- Original topic lessons, learning objectives, practical assignments, deliverables and assessment rubrics.
- Curated links to free-first third-party learning resources.
- Interactive prerequisites, roadmap navigation, theme switching and local progress tracking.
- Community contributions, issue templates and automated validation.

Anyone can run, fork, adapt and contribute to the core under the terms of `LICENSE`.

## Potential paid extensions

Paid functionality should live in separate modules or services and must not be required to access the open-source lessons:
- Structured cohort programmes, live workshops and instructor feedback.
- Expert-reviewed capstone projects and portfolio assessments.
- Mentor matching and career coaching.
- Downloadable premium case-study packs and guides (where the publisher owns the rights.)

These are product proposals, not currently implemented or chargeable features.

## Content and attribution policy

The lesson explanations, assignments and rubrics in this repository are original educational material. External links point to their publishers. A link does not imply endorsement, permission to republish, or a guarantee that a resource remains free.
